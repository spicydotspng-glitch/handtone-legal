The free hour

Every player gets one hour of playing time free. It counts only while the stage plays from the camera in the foreground. The demo and the welcome pages stay free after the hour.

HandTone Pro

HandTone Pro keeps the stage open without a time limit. It is offered as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy, in your local currency.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically at the same price for the same period unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings at any time; cancelling stops the next renewal and Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. The lifetime purchase is a single payment with no renewal.

Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies. Restore purchases (Settings, HandTone Pro) brings Pro back on a new or reset device signed in to the same Apple Account.

Using HandTone

Use the app safely: keep enough space around you while you move your hands, and keep the volume at a comfortable level, especially on headphones. Clips you make are yours.
