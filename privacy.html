Privacy Policy

Updated 10 November 2026

The short version

HandTone does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. HandTone has no servers: the only network traffic is Apple's own, for purchases and iCloud.

The camera

HandTone uses the front camera to see where your hands and fingers are. Each video frame is analysed on your device, in memory, by Apple's on-device hand pose detection, and dropped at once. Video is never saved, never sent anywhere and never used for anything else.

Clips you record

When you tap record, HandTone makes a short video of the light and the sound on your device. Your camera image is only part of a clip if you switch on "Show me in clips" in Settings. Clips stay on your device until you choose to share them or save them to Photos. HandTone asks to add to Photos only when you tap Save to Photos.

Settings and loops

Your tempo, key, voice, beat and display choices are kept on your device only. Loops you record live in memory while you play and are not saved or sent.

Purchases and the free hour

HandTone Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app asks Apple whether a purchase is active on your device.

To count the free hour, HandTone keeps one number, the minutes you have played, in your device's Keychain and in your iCloud key-value storage, so it carries over to your other devices and a reinstall. Apple stores it as part of your iCloud data; it is not sent to us and contains nothing about you.

Children

HandTone does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
