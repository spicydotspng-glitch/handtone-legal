Getting a good sound

Stand or sit about an arm's length from the camera with both hands in view and some light on them. The hand on the left of the screen picks the chord (one to five fingers), the hand on the right adds harmonics. A fist mutes, a clap starts the pulse. Headphones give the full low end; on the built-in speaker the bass is voiced so a small speaker can carry it.

Loops, beats and voices

Bring your palms together slowly and hold them for a second (or tap the red record button) to record a loop after a one bar count in. Tap record again to add a layer, the arrow to undo the last one, the bin to clear. The Voice and Beat buttons at the bottom pick one of six sounds and one of four synthesised beats.

HandTone Pro

Your first hour of playing is free. After that, HandTone Pro keeps the stage open: monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase. Restore a purchase from Settings, HandTone Pro, Restore purchases. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings (Settings, your name, Subscriptions).

The camera does not start

Open Settings, find HandTone and turn on Camera. If another app is using the camera, HandTone waits and carries on when it is free.
