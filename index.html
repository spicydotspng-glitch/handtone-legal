Play chords with your hands

HandTone is an instrument for iPad and iPhone. Raise your hands in front of the front camera: your fingers choose pure, just-intonation chords, and a green phosphor oscilloscope draws the sound in 3D.

Record loops on the bar, add a synthesised beat and pick one of six voices, from the glassy Reel to Choir, Glass and Bass. It runs entirely on your device and needs no account. The first hour of playing is free; HandTone Pro keeps the stage open after that.
